n8n Google Calendar

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This policy describes how the personal application n8n Google Calendar, operated by Brad Wyatt, accesses, uses, stores, and shares Google Calendar data.

Scope and purpose

The application is a personal, self-hosted n8n automation. Its sole intended user is Brad Wyatt. It monitors selected personal and workout calendars for newly created events and uses relevant event details to create corresponding Todoist tasks. It is not a commercial or publicly available service.

Google Calendar data accessed

The automation accesses selected calendars and event records through Google OAuth. Event information made available to the configured workflow may include an event's title, date and time, description, location, calendar information, event link, and other event metadata. The precise fields read or processed have not been independently verified and may vary with the workflow configuration.

How the data is used and shared

Calendar data is used only to detect new events and create related personal tasks. The workflow maps selected event details into a Todoist task. Depending on that mapping, information such as the event title, schedule, description, location, or event link may be sent to Todoist; this list does not claim that every field is transferred. Todoist processes information it receives under its own terms and privacy practices.

Google Calendar data is not sold or used for advertising, profiling, credit decisions, or unrelated purposes.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the personal productivity features described in this policy. Human access is limited to Brad Wyatt, the data owner and application operator, when needed to operate, troubleshoot, or maintain his workflows, or when otherwise required by law.

Local storage and execution history

n8n runs as a Windows service on a computer operated by Brad Wyatt. Depending on the n8n configuration and the outcome of a workflow run, local execution history, logs, inputs, outputs, or event details may be retained by n8n. The current execution-history retention and automatic-pruning settings have not been independently verified.

Authentication and security

Google access is authorized using OAuth credentials managed through n8n; the workflow does not require Brad Wyatt's Google password. n8n is accessed locally at localhost:5678, but this statement is not a guarantee that the service is technically restricted to local access. Protection of the data depends on the security and access settings of the Windows computer, n8n, Google, and Todoist. No specific encryption method or absolute security guarantee is claimed here.

Retention and deletion

No specific retention period or automatic deletion schedule is promised. Locally stored workflow and execution data can be managed through the n8n installation. Tasks already created in Todoist must be managed or deleted in Todoist. Revoking Google access stops future authorized access but does not automatically remove information already stored in n8n or transferred to Todoist.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the third-party connections or security settings of the applicable Google Account. The n8n Google credential may also be disconnected or removed from the local n8n installation.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the application's handling of Google Calendar data materially changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, email brad@BradWyatt.com.
